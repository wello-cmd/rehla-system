# Rehla

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Managers, warehouse staff, dispatchers and finance teams. The user confirmed that both daily operations and executive reporting should benefit from the redesign.

## Product Purpose

Manage commerce operations and understand business performance in one role-based workspace. Success means readable reporting, clear navigation, and efficient routine actions.

## Operating Context

Existing React application with inventory, warehouse scanning, delivery, invoices, expenses, customers, Shopify and Bosta integrations. Financial values are displayed in EGP. Reports support date ranges.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing data, permissions, authentication, workflows and integration behavior. Display unavailable data honestly. Support keyboard access, smaller screens and reduced motion. No fabricated metrics or claims.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Rehla name. The approved visual direction is Dispatch Desk (DESIGN.md).

## Evidence on Hand

Existing interface in frontend/src, with shared layout and UI components. No new brand assets supplied.

## Delivery model confirmed 20 October 2026

Shopify orders are handed to Bosta. Rehla also operates in-house delivery, which is not yet linked to Shopify. Delivery providers are not separate sales channels and must not be counted as independent revenue sources.

## Collection and remittance confirmed

Track customer payment to the courier separately from courier remittance to Rehla. Delivery status alone does not prove remittance. Financial schema and historical reconciliation remain a later, separately specified data pass.
